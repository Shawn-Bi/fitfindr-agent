# FitFindr Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

This policy explains what FitFindr collects, why, who helps us run the service, and
how you can delete your data. FitFindr is for users in the United States.

## 1. What we collect

| Data | Examples | Why we need it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Email address; password (handled by our login provider, never stored by us in plain text) | To create your account and sign you in |
| Profile (optional) | Usual sizes, favorite styles and colors, budget | To filter and rank search results for you |
| Closet | Photos of your clothes; name, category, colors, style tags, size, brand, season, notes | To show your closet and suggest outfits using what you own |
| Searches | What you type, filters you use, items you save | To show results, keep your Saved list, and improve search |
| Choices | Whether you opted in to "Email me new matches" | To respect your choice (we don't send these emails yet) |
| Technical | Basic logs such as time of request and errors | To keep the service working and secure |

We do **not** collect your height, weight or body measurements, and we do **not**
collect payment details. When you upload a photo, we remove location (GPS) and other
hidden metadata before storing it.

## 2. Who can see your data

Your closet, photos and profile are private to you by default. We don't sell your
personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

## 3. Service providers we use

We share data only with companies that help us run FitFindr, and only what they need:

- **Supabase** — stores your account, profile, closet details and photos, and handles sign-in.
- **Google Gemini** — receives your search text and closet item details (not your email) to write outfit suggestions and captions.
- **RapidAPI and its API providers** — receive your search keywords to fetch listings from eBay, Etsy, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari and Facebook Marketplace. They don't receive your email or closet.
- **Vercel** — hosts the website.
- **Render** — hosts our server.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms. Stores you click through to
(eBay, Etsy, and so on) have their own privacy policies; once you leave FitFindr,
their policies apply.

## 4. How long we keep data

We keep your account and closet data until you delete it. Search results from stores
are cached for up to 24 hours. Logs are kept for [LOG RETENTION PERIOD].

## 5. Deleting your data

From your Profile you can delete any single closet item, or delete your whole account.
Deleting your account removes your profile, closet details, photos and saved items from
our systems within [NUMBER] days, except where we must keep something by law.

## 6. Security

We use encrypted connections, keep API keys on our servers only, and limit who can
access data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## 7. Children

FitFindr is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your state (for example, California), you may have the right to access,
correct, or delete your data, or to know what we collect. Email us to make a request.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app or by email.
